What this is, and what it isn't yet

  • Data. NWM v3.0 operational short_range channel_rt files for all 24 cycles on 19 April 2026, plus each cycle's analysis_assim tm00 file, read through CIROH's Kerchunk references on s3://ciroh-nwm-zarr-copy by scripts/pull_nwm_short_range.py in the streamflow_getter fork.
  • “Analysis” is the model's own best estimate after data assimilation, not a gage observation. A difference here shows how far a forecast moves from NWM's later analysis, not how wrong it was against the river.
  • Lead time is counted from the forecast cycle. The analysis files' own reference time is 3 hours earlier than the cycle, so it can't be used for this.
  • The Black Warrior analysis swings several-fold from one hour to the next, and its flows are low late in the day, so its relative differences run large. Worth checking before reading much into them: regulated releases upstream, or gage nudging in the analysis, could each produce swings like these.
  • Two reaches only. River names come from the starting script and haven't been checked against the hydrofabric yet.
  • Next. Add NRDS t-route output for the same reaches and hours (the NWM vs NextGen comparison in the research plan), then USGS gages as observations.
